How this hub replaces the spreadsheet

  • No more double entry. Each initiative is entered once on the Operational Tracker. The My View / 1:1 tab and the CEO Dashboard are generated from it — update a status or a quarterly note in one place and it appears everywhere.
  • 1:1 meetings. Open My View / 1:1, pick a person, and you'll see their plan initiatives plus their monthly check-in log (wins, blockers, focus, wellbeing) — the space for individual progress that sits outside the plan projects.
  • Quarterly updates. Use the period selector on the tracker to view or write each quarter's update against every initiative.
  • The bar at the top is your saving light. Green ✓ means everything saves automatically on this device. Export saves the whole hub to one file; Import loads it back — or merges a colleague's exported file into yours.
  • For permanent saving, keep team-hub.html in a folder on your computer and always open it from there.
