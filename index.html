      Keeping your information safe

      • The green bar at the top is your saving light. Green ✓ means everything you type is being saved automatically on this device. If it ever turns red ⚠️, saving is off for that window — use Export straight away.
      • Export / Import (top right). Export saves everything to one small file in your Downloads. Import loads it back. Do an Export every week or two as a safety net.

      Make saving automatic (recommended for your hub)

      • The most reliable way to keep your info forever is to run this from a file on your own computer:
      • 1. Save the file index.html that Claude sent you into a folder you'll keep (e.g. Documents).
      • 2. Double-click it — it opens in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
      • 3. Bookmark that page. From then on it saves automatically and your info stays put, even after restarting your computer.
      • Keep opening it from the same file in the same folder — that's what lets the browser remember your data.

      Turning on live email & calendar

      • The four buttons up top jump to your real Outlook email, calendar, Microsoft To Do, and Teams. To pull your live inbox and calendar into this dashboard, your IT team needs to approve the “Claude / Microsoft 365” connection (it's currently blocked by a company security policy).
