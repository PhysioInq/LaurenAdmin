      Using your panels

      • To-Do List: type a task and (optionally) pick a due date. The list sorts by what's due soonest and flags anything overdue, today or this week.
      • Meeting Agendas: tap a meeting tab (Team Meeting, or your 1:1s with Jonathan, Pat, Robyn, Marty) and jot points as they come to you. Set a weekly day and it shows the next date. Press Copy agenda to drop it into an email or Teams, and tick items off as you cover them. Use ＋ New for any other meeting.
      • Post-Meeting Actions: paste your notes or a Copilot/Teams transcript (or Upload a .txt/.vtt file) and click Find actions. Add each one to your to-do list or delegate it to a team member in one click. For the smartest read, use Extract with Claude.

      Getting your Copilot transcript out of Teams

      • In Teams: open the meeting → Recap → Transcript → the ⋯ menu → Download and choose .vtt (or just open the transcript, select all the text and copy it).
      • Then paste it into the Post-Meeting Actions box, or use Upload .txt / .vtt.
      • Coming once IT approves the Microsoft connection: I'll be able to pull the transcript straight from the meeting and write the actions for you — no downloading.

      Keeping your information safe

      • The green bar at the top is your saving light. Green ✓ means everything you type is being saved automatically on this device. If it ever turns red ⚠️, saving is off for that window — use Export straight away.
      • Export / Import (top right). Export saves everything to one small file in your Downloads. Import loads it back. Do an Export every week or two as a safety net.

      Make saving automatic (recommended for your hub)

      • The most reliable way to keep your info forever is to run this from a file on your own computer:
      • 1. Save the file index.html that Claude sent you into a folder you'll keep (e.g. Documents).
      • 2. Double-click it — it opens in Microsoft Edge or Chrome.
      • 3. Bookmark that page. From then on it saves automatically and your info stays put, even after restarting your computer.
      • Keep opening it from the same file in the same folder — that's what lets the browser remember your data.

      Turning on live email & calendar
